The executive problem

Risk rarely arrives labelled as risk.

A bank asks for enhanced documentation. A counterparty becomes difficult to verify. A supplier operates through a higher-risk jurisdiction. A regulator asks to see the controls behind a policy. A growing company discovers that nobody actually owns compliance.

These are business problems as much as compliance problems. Blackthorne & Chase helps management teams identify the exposure, make decisions, build practical controls and keep oversight running after the consultant has left.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What does Blackthorne & Chase do?+

Blackthorne & Chase is a Dubai mainland management consultancy providing corporate risk, compliance, sanctions, governance, business resilience and Canada–UAE market-entry advisory.

Do you provide legal advice in the UAE?+

No. Blackthorne & Chase operates as a UAE management consultancy and does not provide UAE legal advice or legal representation. Where UAE legal advice is required, clients are referred to appropriately licensed UAE counsel.

What is a Fractional Chief Compliance Officer?+

It is an outsourced senior compliance advisory function for businesses that need structured oversight without building a full internal department. Scope is tailored to the client's business and regulatory requirements; where a regulator requires a formally approved or registered officer, the appropriate requirements are addressed separately.

Can you help a Canadian company enter the UAE?+

Yes. We provide Canada–UAE market-entry and corporate advisory, including readiness, governance, compliance and coordination with specialist providers where regulated or legal services are required.
