Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about compliance in the UAE

What is a Fractional CCO and does my Dubai business need one?

A Fractional Chief Compliance Officer is an outsourced compliance function that manages your compliance program on a retainer basis. Under Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025, having a dedicated compliance function is no longer optional for many regulated businesses. A Fractional CCO gives you senior-level compliance oversight at a fraction of the cost of a full-time hire — typically AED 300,000–500,000 per year in-house. Our service starts at AED 4,500 per month.

Which businesses in Dubai are required to have an AML compliance program?

Under UAE AML law, all Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions (DNFBPs) are required to have a functioning AML compliance program. This includes real estate brokers and agents, accountants, auditors, corporate service providers, precious metals and gemstones dealers, and virtual asset service providers. There is no size exemption. The Ministry of Economy imposed over AED 130 million in fines on non-compliant DNFBPs since 2022, with AED 42 million in H1 2025 alone.

How much does an AML compliance program cost for a Dubai SME?

Blackthorne & Chase offers fixed-fee AML compliance programs starting at AED 7,500 for AML Essentials, which includes goAML registration, an Institutional Risk Assessment, an AML Policy Manual, CDD/EDD procedures, and staff training. AML Complete is AED 14,500 for full-scope implementation including gap analysis, risk matrix, and inspection readiness preparation. All prices are fixed — no hourly billing.

How quickly can you set up an AML compliance program?

AML Essentials is delivered in 1–2 weeks. AML Complete, which includes a comprehensive gap analysis and inspection readiness report, takes 2–3 weeks from engagement. Both are fixed-fee with clear delivery timelines agreed upfront.

Does Blackthorne & Chase help with business setup and compliance in the UAE?

Yes. We advise businesses on their compliance requirements from the point of incorporation — including jurisdiction selection (mainland, DIFC, ADGM, or free zone), structure advisory, AML obligations upon licensing, and governance framework design. This is business and regulatory advisory; where a legal opinion is required, we work alongside your licensed UAE legal counsel.

Can a template AML policy I found online pass a UAE regulatory inspection?

Unlikely. UAE regulators review AML programs in detail during inspections. A generic template that doesn't reflect your specific business type, risk profile, customer base, and regulatory category will typically fail inspection. Every AML program we build is customized to your specific business and structured to address the inspection criteria applied by the Ministry of Economy and relevant UAE supervisory authorities.
